# Frequently Asked Questions — Down To The Last Detailing

## Is Down To The Last Detailing a mobile service?
Yes. Down To The Last Detailing is a fully mobile detailing service. We come to the customer's location to perform the service.

## What are your hours?
We are open every day, Sunday through Saturday, from 8am to 7pm.

## How do I book an appointment?
All appointments must be booked in advance. You can book online through the booking page on our website or by phone.

## Is a deposit required?
A deposit may be required at the time of booking for certain services, such as ceramic coating.

## When is payment due?
Full payment is due when the service is completed, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## What payment methods do you accept?
Please contact us to confirm accepted payment methods.

## Why does pricing vary?
All listed prices are starting prices. Final pricing depends on the make, model, and condition of the vehicle. Every vehicle is inspected before work begins. Once a booking is confirmed, the quoted price is kept.

## What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations must be made in advance to avoid charges. Deposits may be non-refundable for no-shows or late cancellations. Please contact us to confirm the exact cancellation notice required.

## Do you offer refunds?
Refunds are considered only in cases of clear service failure.

## What should I do before my appointment?
Please remove all personal belongings from the vehicle before the service. Let us know about any pre-existing damage, modifications, or issues with the vehicle before work begins.

## Are your products safe for my car's paint?
Yes. We use non-abrasive, pH-neutral products that are safe for all paint types.

## How should I care for my car after a ceramic coating?
The coating needs time to cure. Do not expose the vehicle to rain or wash it for a period after application. We will give you the exact curing time and maintenance guidelines after the service. Following them helps protect the coating and any warranty.

## Does ceramic coating remove scratches and swirl marks?
Ceramic coating protects paint but does not fully correct it. Some minor imperfections may remain unless paint correction is also purchased.

## What if the weather is bad on my appointment day?
Because we work outdoors at your location, appointments may be delayed or rescheduled due to weather.

## What if I have concerns after the service?
Please inspect your vehicle when the service is finished and let us know about any concerns right away.
