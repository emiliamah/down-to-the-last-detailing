# Services and Pricing — Down To The Last Detailing

Down To The Last Detailing is a mobile detailing service. All prices are starting prices. Final pricing varies depending on the make and model of the vehicle. Appointments are booked online through the website's booking page.

## Interior Car Wash
- Price: starting at $30
- Duration: about 30 minutes
- Includes: vacuuming and wiping down the interior with towels and interior cleaner. Different products are used depending on the interior material.

## Exterior Car Wash
- Price: starting at $30
- Duration: about 30 minutes
- Includes: exterior wash with soap and spotless water, wheel scrubbing, tire dressing, and window wipe-down.

## Regular Interior/Exterior Car Wash
- Price: starting at $60
- Duration: about 1 hour
- Includes: everything in the Interior Car Wash and Exterior Car Wash combined: interior vacuum and wipe-down, exterior wash with spotless water, wheel scrubbing, tire dressing, and window wipe-down.

## Steam Cleaning (Interior)
- Price: starting at $125
- Duration: about 2 hours
- Includes: interior deep vacuuming, advanced interior sanitization using professional-grade dry vapor steam (minimal water and extreme heat to dissolve grime and sanitize surfaces), material-specific conditioning for leather and vinyl, light stain removal, and streak-free interior glass.

## Interior Detail
- Price: starting at $150
- Duration: about 2 hours
- Includes: thorough cleaning of AC vents and all nooks and crannies, thorough interior and trunk vacuum, interior and door jamb wipe-down, interior window cleaning, and leather cleaning and conditioning.

## Full Exterior Detail
- Price: starting at $135
- Duration: about 1 hour 30 minutes
- Includes: exterior hand wash with spotless water, clay bar paint decontamination, premium wax application, wheel deep clean and tire shine, interior vacuum and wipe-down, material-specific conditioning for leather and vinyl, light stain removal, and streak-free glass.

## Premium Interior/Exterior Detail
- Price: starting at $275
- Duration: about 2 hours 30 minutes
- Includes: thorough interior and trunk vacuum, interior and door jamb wipe-down, interior window cleaning, leather cleaning and conditioning, interior steam cleaning, full exterior hand wash, tire dressing, rim cleaning, exterior window cleaning, complete wax for protection and shine, clay bar paint treatment, exterior plastic dressing, and a single-stage buff.

## Ceramic Coating
- Price: starting at $700
- Duration: about 5 hours 30 minutes
- Includes: paint decontamination (iron removal and clay bar treatment), color correction, multi-step polishing, and a ceramic coating that provides three to ten years of paint protection.
